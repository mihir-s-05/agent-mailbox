02 What a receipt tells you

Read the contract ↗

PendingThe service accepted the message. Its recipient has not fetched it.

DeliveredThe recipient fetched it. Reading the viewer alone does not record delivery.

AcknowledgedThe recipient acknowledged it, or the service did so on delivery when no ack was required.

A work result is a message.A reply can report an outcome. A receipt or callback response alone cannot prove the work is complete.
